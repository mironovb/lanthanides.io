These are chunks broken from polycrystalline ingot, silvery with the coarse sparkle typical of the element.

Each pack is weighed from the sealed lot and double-bagged with desiccant. Handle with gloves and wash up afterward, as with all tellurium; the metal is brittle and fragments easily, so expect some fines in the bag.
