Thulium is the least common of the stable rare earths that still reaches the open market as metal. This lot is dendritic material at 99.9%, the branching crystal habit left by vacuum distillation, with bright faces that dull only slowly when kept dry. Packs are split from the sealed lot under argon and re-sealed to the stated mass.

Each pack ships in an argon-flushed barrier bag inside a rigid mailer, with the lot number printed on the inner seal. Suited to element collections, spectroscopy reference material, and small alloying trials; not offered in bulk volumes.
