A boxed set of four strategic-metal specimens from stock: scandium granules (5 g), thulium dendrites (5 g), tellurium chunks (25 g), and a single bismuth chunk (15 g), each heat-sealed in its own labeled bag. The set exists for side-by-side study; the four metals span the range from reactive rare earth to stable semimetal and make the density and luster differences easy to show.

Specimens sit in a foam-lined presentation case with a printed contents card. Masses are nominal and weighed at packing; the bismuth piece is a single chunk, the rest are poured fills.
