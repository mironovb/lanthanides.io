Nord Element OÜ is a laboratory-materials trader based in Tallinn, Estonia.
The desk buys sealed refinery and distributor lots of research metals, splits
them under inert gas where a listing calls for it, and re-packs to the stated
mass with the lot number printed on the inner seal. Lots that arrive with
supplier paperwork keep that paperwork attached to the listing; lots that do
not are listed as seller-declared.

The stock list leans toward rare earths and chalcogens in laboratory
quantities rather than bulk industrial volumes. Orders ship from Tallinn in
plain packaging.
