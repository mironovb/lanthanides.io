I have been selling rare-earth and specialty-metal samples at retail for
about six years — roughly ten thousand orders shipped, one element at a
time. The catalog here is the same one I run at my own store: high-purity
metals bagged or vialed from stock, weighed, sealed against air and
moisture, and labeled with what they are and where they came from.

Most of my material is refined metal — scandium, terbium, and the
workhorse strategic metals — plus a few classic low-melt and reagent
alloys. Where a listing states an origin, that is what my own records
say; where it does not, I would rather leave the field empty than guess.
Purity figures are the grades I buy against. None of it has been
independently assayed for this marketplace yet, and every listing says
so until that changes.

If you want a specific pack size, a photo of the exact piece you would
receive, or paperwork for an institutional purchase, write to me before
ordering — that has always been how most of my sales work anyway.
